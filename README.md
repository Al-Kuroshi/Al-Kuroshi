## `> history --human`

History fascinates me because it's more than dates, events, and names. I love trying to understand the human side of it: how people actually lived, what they enjoyed, what they disliked, what they believed, and how they dealt with the ordinary problems of their time.

I'm interested in the details that make the past feel human.

## `> philosophy --engineering`

I consider engineering another form of art, and art itself a form of self-expression.

Every problem I solve, every system I design, and every framework I build carries a little of me in it. The choices, trade-offs, and details make the result uniquely its own.

For me, perfection isn't an end goal. Refining, experimenting, learning, and making something better is the process — and the process itself is the art.
